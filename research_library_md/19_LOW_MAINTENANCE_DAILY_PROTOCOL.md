# 19 — Low-Maintenance Daily Protocol

> Part of the **Men's Transformation Research Library**. This is the "minimum effective effort" file: the simplest routine that captures most of the proven benefit.

---

## 1. Executive summary

- **80% of the result comes from 5 behaviours**: protein at every meal, resistance training 2–3×/week, 8,000–12,000 steps/day, 7–9 h sleep, and no liquid calories. Everything else is refinement.
- The protocol below requires **≈20 minutes/day of active effort** (plus training sessions) and **no daily calorie counting**.
- **Decision fatigue is the real enemy**: the protocol works by *removing* decisions (same breakfast, same 5 meals, same training template, same weekly review) rather than by adding discipline.
- **Tracking should be minimal and periodic**: weight trend continuously (it's free and automatic), food only in 2-week calibration blocks, waist monthly.
- **Low maintenance ≠ no structure.** The structure is small, written down, and permanent.

---

## 3. Evidence quality

**High** for the core five behaviours; **Moderate** for the specific dosing/frequency choices; **Low-Moderate** for the behavioural scaffolding (habit stacking, environment design) — which is nonetheless what makes the physiology achievable.

---

## 4. Effectiveness (expected magnitude)

A man at 95 kg, 25% body fat, following the protocol without calorie counting:
- **Months 1–3:** −4 to −7 kg, waist −4 to −6 cm, strength +20–40%.
- **Months 4–6:** −3 to −5 kg more; visible face/waist change.
- **Month 12:** 10–15% of starting weight lost, most of it fat, in ~20 min/day of effort.
- **Time cost:** ~3.5 h/week total (3 × 40 min training + 30 min walking daily, of which most is incidental).

---

## 5. Fastest reasonably safe approach

If you want to accelerate without adding complexity, add **only** these, in order: (1) a defined eating window (14:10); (2) an explicit 20% deficit for 8–12 weeks; (3) a third training session; (4) 12,000 steps. Do not add more than one at a time, and only if the previous four weeks were ≥85% consistent.

---

## 6. Free and cheap alternatives

Everything in this protocol is free except shoes and (optionally) resistance bands. No app, subscription, supplement or device is required. Delete every paid "protocol" product from your consideration until this routine has run for 12 weeks.

---

## 7. Practical protocol

### The daily minimum (≈20 min active effort)

| Time | Action | Time cost |
|---|---|---|
| On waking | Same time daily; bright light; 250–500 ml water | 2 min |
| Breakfast | **Same meal every day**, 30–40 g protein (e.g., 3 eggs + yoghurt, or oats + whey) | 5–8 min |
| Midday | 10-min walk after lunch | 10 min |
| Lunch | Protein palm-and-a-half + vegetables half plate + one starch | 10 min prep |
| Afternoon | Water/coffee only; no snacks by default | 0 |
| Evening | Protein + vegetables + starch; **kitchen closed 3 h before bed** | 15 min prep |
| Any time | 8,000–12,000 steps total (walk calls, stairs, one deliberate walk) | 30 min incidental |
| Before bed | Screens off 45 min before; phone outside bedroom | 0 |

### The weekly minimum
- **2–3 resistance sessions** (A/B full-body template, file 03).
- **One 5-minute review:** weight average, training sessions, step average, alcohol units.
- **One grocery run** using the 4-item list (protein, vegetables, fruit, fibre base).

### The monthly minimum
- Waist measurement + photos in the same light.
- Check the 7-day weight average against your maintenance range (file 06).
- Restock creatine/psyllium/vitamin D.

### What you deliberately do NOT do
- No daily calorie counting (except 2-week calibration blocks).
- No macro splitting beyond protein.
- No meal timing beyond "not late at night".
- No weighing food forever.
- No supplement stacks.
- No daily scale panic.

### Decision-fatigue eliminators (the actual mechanism)
1. **One repeated breakfast** (removes decision #1).
2. **Five rotating dinners** (removes decision #2).
3. **A fixed grocery list** (removes decision #3).
4. **A fixed training template** (removes decision #4).
5. **A fixed weekly review time** (removes decision #5).
6. **A written rule for exceptions** ("If I eat out, I order a protein + vegetables and skip dessert/drinks").

---

## 8. Low-maintenance version (the "if everything is falling apart" version)

**Three rules only:**
1. Protein at every meal.
2. Walk 30 minutes a day.
3. Two lifting sessions a week.

This is enough to hold weight and most of your muscle during the busiest months of your life. Add rules back when life calms down. **Never abandon the three.**

---

## 9. Safety and contraindications

- See files 02, 03, 10 and 11 for specific contraindications (medical clearance before aggressive deficits or intense training; protein restrictions in kidney disease; sleep apnoea screening).
- **If the "low-maintenance" version is producing rapid unintentional weight loss, fatigue, or low mood**, get assessed — that is not the protocol working.
- **Do not use this protocol to normalise disordered eating** (rigid rules + guilt). If food rules cause distress, speak to a professional.

---

## 10. Products and tools

Optional and minimal: a scale, a tape measure, shoes, resistance bands. Everything else in file 18 is discretionary. **The protocol must work with zero purchases.**

---

## 11. Common mistakes

1. **Adding complexity when adherence fails** (the instinct is wrong — simplify instead).
2. **Optimising meal timing before hitting protein.**
3. **Tracking everything forever** — burnout, then abandonment.
4. **No written rules** — decisions get made by the hungriest version of you.
5. **All-or-nothing weeks** ("I missed Monday, the week is ruined").
6. **A protocol that requires 90 minutes/day** — it will not survive a busy month.
7. **Chasing perfection in one domain while ignoring sleep.**
8. **Rebuilding the plan every 2 weeks** instead of repeating it.
9. **Ignoring the weekly review** — the only part that catches drift.
10. **Believing low-maintenance means no monitoring.**

---

## 12. Final recommendations

1. **Five behaviours**: protein, lift, walk, sleep, no liquid calories.
2. **Write the rules down** and repeat the same meals/training.
3. **Track weight (automatic) and waist (monthly)**; count calories only in 2-week blocks.
4. **20 minutes a day** is the entire ask.
5. **Have a three-rule emergency version** for chaotic months.
6. **Review weekly for five minutes** — that is the whole maintenance system.
