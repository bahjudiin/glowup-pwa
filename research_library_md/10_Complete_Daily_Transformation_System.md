# 10 — The Complete Daily Transformation System

*Phase 2 module, file 10 of 12. This is the operating manual that ties files `01`–`09` together. Nothing new is claimed here; every mechanism is sourced in its own file.*

---

## 1. How to use this file

There are **three versions**:

| Version | For whom | Daily effort | Expected rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Minimum-effort** | Busy, low motivation, or first month | ~15 min/day | Slow, very sustainable |
| **B. Faster fat-loss** | Already consistent for 3–4 weeks, sleeping and eating protein well | ~45 min/day | Faster, still safe |
| **C. Maintenance** | At or near goal weight | ~15 min/day, forever | Zero loss, zero regain |

**Start with A. Move to B only when A is boring.** Move to C when you hit your goal and stay there for a year.

**Non-negotiable safety rule for all three versions:** if you develop chest pain, fainting, confusion, palpitations, severe weakness, vomiting that prevents drinking, rapid swelling, or thoughts of self-harm — **stop the plan and seek medical care.** No schedule in this file is worth pushing through a warning sign, and no version of this plan tells you to.

## 2. Version A — Minimum-effort (the default)

**Target: 3 habits. That's all.**

### 2.1 Morning (5 minutes)
| Time | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|
| On waking | Water (300–500 ml). Open curtains / step outside 10 min. | Rehydration + circadian anchoring (`05` §5) |
| Within 30 min | **Protein breakfast OR deliberately skip it** — your choice, consistently | Protein stabilises appetite (`02` §5.2) |
| Before leaving | Decide **when your eating window closes tonight** | Prevents late-night eating (the main food failure point) |

### 2.2 Eating window (the only rules)
1. **Protein at every meal** — a palm-sized portion (25–40 g).
2. **Half the plate is vegetables**, eaten first.
3. **No calorie-containing drinks.** Water, plain tea, black coffee.
4. **Slowest meal of the day takes 20 minutes.**

### 2.3 Fasting window
- **Default: 12:12 – 14:10.** Nothing after ~3 h before bed. Water/tea/black coffee only.
- No fasting required at all in the first two weeks if it makes you miserable. **Closing the kitchen after dinner counts.**

### 2.4 Evening (5 minutes)
1. Last meal 2–3 h before bed.
2. Last caffeine 8 h before bed.
3. Prepare tomorrow's protein (boil eggs / defrost chicken / soak lentils). This single act prevents most next-day failures.
4. Same bedtime every night.

### 2.5 Weekly (10 minutes)
- Weigh daily (or ≥ 4×/week); write down **only the 7-day average**.
- Waist once, same spot.
- 2 × 25-minute bodyweight sessions (`06` §4).
- Groceries: cabbage, tomatoes, onions, eggplant, carrots, eggs, chicken/fish, potatoes, lentils, seasonal fruit.

**Version A requires no supplements, no app, no scale, no gym.**

## 3. Version B — Faster fat-loss (after 3–4 consistent weeks)

Everything in A, plus the following. Only adopt it if A is routine and sleep is ≥ 7 h.

### 3.1 Morning
- Same as A, plus: **step target 8,000–10,000/day** (walk in the cool hours).
- Resistance training **3×/week**, 30 min, progressive (`06` §4).

### 3.2 Eating window
- **14:10 or 16:8** (never shorter than 8 h).
- **Three protein feedings of 30–40 g** inside the window.
- Fibre **25–35 g/day** from food; consider psyllium only if constipated.
- **One planned "treat" per week**, inside the window, in a portion you decide in advance.

### 3.3 Fasting window
- Water, plain tea, black coffee. **No sweetened anything.**
- If you train, train **fed** or at least not 20 h fasted.
- **One optional 24 h fast per week maximum** — only if you have none of the contraindications in `03` §8.1, and only after a month of consistency.

### 3.4 Evening
- Same as A. **Protect sleep above everything** — it determines tomorrow's hunger.
- No hard training after ~2 h before bed if it disrupts your sleep.

### 3.5 Weekly
- Full tracking: weekly average weight, waist, sessions completed, protein hit (yes/no), sleep average.
- **Diet break** at maintenance for 1 week every 8–12 weeks, or earlier if hunger/fatigue climbs (`08` §5).

**What B deliberately excludes:** very-low-calorie days, 20:4/OMAD, 36–48 h fasts as routine, fat burners, and "double cardio" to fix a stall.

## 4. Version C — Maintenance (the version that actually matters)

**The goal changes from "lose" to "hold."** No deficit unless the trigger rule fires.

| Element | Maintenance version |
|---|---|
| **Morning** | Water, daylight, protein breakfast, fixed wake time |
| **Eating** | Same meals as B, **at maintenance amounts** (roughly +10–20% over your deficit intake). Protein stays at **110–140 g/day** |
| **Fasting** | 12:12–14:10 continues — it is the structure that prevents late-night drift |
| **Training** | **2 sessions/week minimum, permanently.** This is what keeps the body composition you paid for |
| **Steps** | 7,000–10,000/day |
| **Sleep** | 7–9 h, same window |
| **Monitoring** | Weigh daily, **weekly average only**; waist weekly |
| **Band** | ±2 kg around target. Inside the band → **do nothing** |
| **Trigger** | 2 consecutive weekly averages above the band → modest deficit (10% below maintenance) for 2–4 weeks, then back to maintenance |
| **Review** | Monthly, one page (`08` §6.3) |

**Why this is necessary:** hunger hormones remain unfavourable after weight loss ([Sumithran 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22029981/)), regain is the default in long-term trials ([Look AHEAD](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24307184/)), and stopping active treatment causes rapid regain ([SURMOUNT-4](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38078870/)). Maintenance is a skill, not a state.

## 5. Decision tree — seven problems, seven responses

**Read the problem, follow the branch, do the smallest sufficient thing.** Never escalate the plan in response to a symptom.

### 5.1 Excessive hunger
1. **Is it constant, with low mood, poor sleep, cold intolerance, hair loss, or loss of libido?**
   → **Yes: you are under-eating.** Stop the deficit, eat at maintenance for 1–2 weeks, and see a doctor if it persists.
2. **Is it only at night?** → Protein at lunch is too low; save 300–400 kcal for the evening meal; close the kitchen.
3. **Is it all day, every day?** → Check protein (should be 110–140 g), fibre (25–35 g), vegetables (400 g/meal), sleep (< 7 h?). Fix these **before** reducing calories further.
4. **Is it after a hard training day or a long fast?** → Eat more on those days; shorten the fast.
5. **Still hungry after all of the above?** → Your deficit is too large. **Reduce it.** A slower loss you can sustain beats a fast one you abandon.

### 5.2 Low energy
1. **Sleep < 7 h?** → Fix sleep first. Nothing else will work.
2. **Deficit > ~1% bodyweight/week?** → Slow down.
3. **Training hard + fasting + deficit?** → Pick two. Train fed or reduce the deficit.
4. **Dizzy on standing, palpitations, breathless on stairs?** → **Eat, hydrate, and see a doctor.** Do not "push through."
5. **Persistent fatigue for > 2 weeks with no explanation?** → Bloodwork/medical review (Phase-1 file `25`).

### 5.3 Poor sleep
1. **Caffeine after 14:00?** → Cut it. Caffeine 6 h before bed measurably disrupts sleep ([Drake 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24235903/)).
2. **Last meal < 2 h before bed, or a large late meal?** → Move it.
3. **Eating window ends late?** → Shift it earlier (`03` §5).
4. **Alcohol in the evening?** → Remove it.
5. **Loud snoring + daytime sleepiness + witnessed pauses?** → **STOP-BANG screen and medical assessment for OSA** (`05` §4). This is not a diet problem.
6. **Waking 3–4 a.m. with hunger?** → Larger protein/fibre at dinner; consider breaking the fast earlier.

### 5.4 Plateau (no change 3 weeks)
1. **Waist still shrinking, or clothes looser?** → Not a plateau. Change nothing (`08` §2).
2. **Adherence genuinely 100% for 3 weeks?** → Recalculate maintenance for your new weight; reduce intake ~10%; add 2,000 steps.
3. **Adherence not 100%?** → Fix that for 2 weeks before changing anything. (This is the answer most of the time.)
4. **Sleep or stress recently worse?** → Fix those first.
5. **Never:** cut to a very low intake, add two cardio sessions, buy a fat burner.

### 5.5 Constipation
1. **Water adequate?** → Drink.
2. **Fibre increased fast?** → Hold at current level, add water, walk.
3. **Try psyllium 5 g/day** with a full glass, 2 h from medication.
4. **> 1 week, or painful, or blood, or severe bloating with vomiting?** → **See a doctor.**
5. **Never:** stimulant laxatives as a routine, "detox" teas, or enemas.

### 5.6 Overeating / binge after fasting
1. **Shorten or drop the fast.** This is the fix, not more discipline (`04` §6).
2. **Eat protein at the start of the eating window.**
3. **Slow the first meal down** — 20 minutes, at a table.
4. **If it repeats, or involves guilt/purging/secrecy:** stop fasting entirely and seek professional help. Diet advice will not solve this.

### 5.7 Fasting intolerance
Signs: headache, irritability, dizziness, cold sweats, poor sleep every fast day, binge at the break, obsession with the clock.
1. **Shorten to 12:12.** That is still effective (`03` §3.1).
2. **Hydrate and add a pinch of salt if sweating.**
3. **Shift the window earlier** rather than later.
4. **If symptoms are severe, or you are on glucose-lowering or BP medication:** stop fasting and speak to your doctor. Fasting is optional; the deficit is not.

## 7. Summary

### 7.1 Strongest verified findings behind this system
1. **Protein + fibre + volume + sleep** are the four levers with the strongest human evidence for reducing spontaneous intake (`02` §§3.3–3.10).
2. **12:12–14:10 captures the benefit of time restriction at the lowest cost**; longer windows are not superior (`03` §3.1).
3. **Resistance training + adequate protein preserves lean mass** ([Garthe 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21558571/); [Morton 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28698222/)).
4. **Maintenance requires permanent structure**, because appetite hormones stay altered ([Sumithran 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22029981/)) and regain is the default without it ([Look AHEAD](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24307184/)).
5. **Most "plateaus" are water, adherence, or measurement noise** ([`08` §2](08_Plateaus_and_Weight_Maintenance.md)).

### 7.2 Most practical actions
Run Version A for a month. Three habits: protein at every meal, kitchen closes 3 h before bed, fixed sleep window. Add walking and two training sessions. Only then consider Version B.

### 7.3 Cheapest effective options
Everything in Version A is free apart from food. The only justified purchase is a kitchen scale.

### 7.4 What is unnecessary
Apps, wearables, supplements, extended fasting, 5 daily meals, meal replacements, calorie cycling, "metabolic reset" protocols, and any purchase in `09` §5.6.

### 7.5 What is unsupported or misleading
- "You must eat 6 small meals to keep your metabolism up" — no.
- "You must fast 16 h for autophagy/fat loss" — no (`03` §4.5).
- "You must track every calorie forever" — short tracking periods are enough.
- "Feeling bad means it's working" — **the opposite.** Symptoms are data, not virtue.

### 7.6 Important risks
Pushing through warning signs (the one thing this system explicitly forbids); fasting on diabetes/BP medication without advice; eating-disorder escalation from rigid rules; dehydration in heat; over-training in a deficit; interpreting water-weight swings as failure.

### 7.7 Concise action plan
1. **Today:** set a wake time and an eating-window close time.
2. **Tomorrow:** cook protein for three days; buy vegetables for three days.
3. **This week:** two 25-minute bodyweight sessions; weigh daily; measure waist once.
4. **In 4 weeks:** if consistent, move to Version B. If not, stay on A — A is not a beginner version, it is the sustainable version.
5. **At goal:** switch to Version C and keep it for a year before deciding anything else.
