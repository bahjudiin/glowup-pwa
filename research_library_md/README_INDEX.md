## Important labels used in this library
- **Nutrition values:** USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy, per 100 g. Food names link to an FDC **search** by FDC ID (USDA has no verifiable per-record deep link). Values are ±10–15% vs local Somali produce.
- **Local prices:** labelled **Verified local price** / **Estimated price** / **Unknown**. **No Somali price in this library is "Verified local price"** — they are third-party Estimated figures or Unknown. Record your own purchases to create verified entries.
- **Products:** iHerb prices marked ✅ were checked live on **1 Nov 2026**; they exclude international shipping, customs and local markup. Marketplace **search** links are labelled as such — no product URLs were invented.
- **Evidence:** every important claim links to PubMed. Mechanistic findings are never presented as proof of real-world outcomes.

## Disclaimer
Research synthesis for information only — not medical advice. Fasting is not appropriate for people with type 1 diabetes, insulin/sulfonylurea-treated diabetes, pregnancy/breastfeeding, eating-disorder history, kidney disease, or those on blood-pressure/diuretic medication without medical advice. Never push through chest pain, fainting, confusion, palpitations or severe weakness.
